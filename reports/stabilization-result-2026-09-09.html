Release boundary

Browser reliability

Interruptions have explicit ownership

Menus, native dialogs, and hidden pages hold separate pause reasons. Closing one cannot resume a still-paused lesson. Speech errors expose an adult read-aloud cue while retaining the full answer gate. Save failures appear on the menu control and in its details.

Combat and casting still resume only from the previous stable checkpoint after reload. Exact mid-combat persistence remains a separate design decision.
Native UI & motion

Browser primitives carry more responsibility

Native dialog shells provide the top layer for menus, Options, deck/pile inspection, and card choices. Focus handling is retained. Card-flight cancellation or missing completion callbacks cannot hold the hand lock indefinitely; reduced-motion users skip card flight.

Existing hand scrolling, pinch zoom, safe areas, and battlefield anchors are preserved.
