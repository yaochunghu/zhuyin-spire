What still needs people

  1. Follow the device and family test checklist, recording device/browser versions and pass/fail evidence.
  2. Block release for cast bypasses, lost stable saves, accidental plays or inaccessible controls. Observe Act I completion or death with casting on.
  3. After those gates pass, plan 3–6 relics for treasure and elites. Potions, events and further expansions remain deferred.
